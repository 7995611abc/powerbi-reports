## Business recommendations

1. Investigate which high-revenue products have weaker margins before increasing discounts.
2. Examine the stronger region’s product mix and evaluate whether successful tactics transfer to other regions.
3. Review monthly seasonality and use the findings to plan inventory and campaigns.
4. Compare customer segments by revenue and profitability before changing acquisition spend.

## Analysis limitations

This dataset is generated for training. Correlations and recommendations are illustrative, not evidence about a real retailer. Profit equals modeled net revenue minus modeled cost; no tax, returns, shipping or overheads are modeled.

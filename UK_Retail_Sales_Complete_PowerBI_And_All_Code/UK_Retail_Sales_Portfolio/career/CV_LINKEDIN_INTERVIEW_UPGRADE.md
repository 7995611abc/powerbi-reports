## 45-second interview introduction
“This was an independent analytics case study using synthetic retail orders across two years. My business question was where sales and profitability differ by product, category and region. I checked row quality, used SQL and Python to aggregate results, and built dashboards in Excel, Power BI and Tableau. I verified all tools against the same baseline: about £1.60 million in sales and a 25.94% profit margin. Technology leads by revenue and North is the strongest region. I explicitly describe this as a simulation, so I don't present findings as real commercial impact.”

## Interview questions & talking points
1. **Why this project?** Shows a full analyst workflow using one consistent business brief and dataset.
2. **How did you verify accuracy?** Compared order count, revenue, profit, and margin across tools with filters cleared.
3. **Why distinct orders?** If multiple line items existed, row counts might overstate orders; use `COUNTD`/`DISTINCTCOUNT` as appropriate.
4. **What is profit margin?** Total profit / total sales, not average of row margins.
5. **Most important insight?** Sales leadership does not guarantee highest profitability; examine margin and mix together.
6. **What would you change with real data?** Validate source systems, returns, costs, date definitions, missingness and customer privacy, then test recommendations against business outcomes.
7. **Limitations?** Synthetic data, no causal inference, simplified order-level model.
